Candle care

Light them well.

A few small habits keep every flame bright and every home safe.

  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Place on a heat-resistant surface, away from curtains and drafts.
  • Keep away from children and pets.
  • Trim the wick before relighting for a clean, even flame.
  • Mithai candles are decorative only. Not edible.
